# eGym Lokhandwala — Environmental Graphics Plan

> The full re-theme: ~18 pieces that flood the gym and the website with one iron creed.
> House style = Mighty-Mick's-of-Mumbai. Brick walls, hand-painted canvas-red banners,
> paper-cream lettering, tobacco black, brass plates, chalk dust, the FIST. Display type
> is **Alfa Slab One** (hammered, not typed); decals/IDs are **JetBrains Mono** uppercase
> at `0.25–0.4em` tracking; body is **Inter**. Every word is Robin's real word or pure
> iron-creed — nothing about him is invented.
>
> **GYM NAME RULE:** the gym is **eGym** ("eGym Lokhandwala · Andheri W · Mumbai"). The
> mark is the wordmark in `/brand/egym/logo/egym-wordmark.svg` — lowercase `e` in
> `mighty.red`, `Gym` in `rocky.paper`. **Never use "GYMROB"** here — that is Robin's
> separate personal brand. Most pieces are name-light (small eGym footer only).

---

## ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEM (the through-line)

1. **One creed, one place.** Every wall says the same thing in a different voice — "iron is religion, show up, never quit" — so a member walking the floor reads the whole manifesto in fragments. The room is the manifesto.
2. **Three poster archetypes, rotated for rhythm:** (A) **TYPE-DRIVEN creed posters** — huge painted slab words on canvas-red or paper-cream, no image; (B) **FIST-EMBLEM posters** — the clenched fist (the Stallone "fists up" symbol) as the single hero element on dark; (C) **TRANSFORMATION-story posters** — `120 → 78`, `FORGED AT 40`, his quote — numerals + verse as the hero. Walk the floor and you hit A-B-C-A-B-C, never two of a kind side by side.
3. **Palette discipline:** canvas-red `#a4271f` ground, paper-cream `#fefae0` letters, tobacco-black `#0e0a07` ink, brass `#b8862b` for plaques/wayfinding, leather `#b49676` for warmth. The cool ring-blue `#4687a6` is used **once per room** (on the boxing-section sign only). Never pure white — always paper-cream.
4. **When eGym is named:** ONLY on welcome/identity, wayfinding, house-rules, and digital pieces (a small painted footer or brass plate). Pure motivational/iron-creed pieces stay **unbranded** — at most a tiny `· eGym · LOKHANDWALA ·` mono decal in a bottom corner, so the creed reads as universal, not as advertising.

## THE SET — 18 PIECES

### 01 · THE MANIFESTO WALL — "I AM A WARRIOR" (centerpiece)
- **Type / size:** Hero wall mural, landscape, ~3:2, full-wall (the room's altar — facing the entrance or the heavy-bag wall).
- **Headline:** `I AM A WARRIOR`
- **Sub-copy (the full verse, painted in fight-card stack):**
  > FOR ME IRON IS A RELIGION
  > AND I AM DEFINED BY ITS TEACHINGS.
  > IT TEACHES HUMILITY · PATIENCE · FOCUS · POWER · BROTHERHOOD.
  > I AM PREPARED TO NEVER QUIT —
  > TO NEVER SAY "I CAN'T DO IT."
- **Decal/footer:** tiny mono, lower-right: `· eGym · LOKHANDWALA · MUMBAI · IRON IS RELIGION ·`
- **Treatment:** Archetype A. Hand-painted directly onto a `mighty.red` canvas field stretched across painted-concrete/brick. `I AM A WARRIOR` enormous in paper-cream Alfa Slab One with paint-bleed and weathering; the verse smaller beneath, ragged-centered, the FIVE words spaced on their own line as a brass-dot rhythm. Two iron bolts top corners, a faint boxer-silhouette in the top-left corner echoing the original Mighty-Mick's banner.
- **Naming:** Unbranded creed (tiny eGym footer only). This is *his* verse, so it is the soul of the room, not an ad.

### 02 · IRON IS RELIGION
- **Type / size:** Wall poster, A0 portrait (or painted column), 2:3.
- **Headline:** `IRON IS RELIGION`
- **Sub-copy:** none — the two words carry it. Optional hairline mono base: `· OLD-SCHOOL IRON ·`
- **Decal/footer:** none, or whisper-small `· eGym ·` bottom-center.
- **Treatment:** Archetype A. Massive stacked slab — `IRON` / `IS` / `RELIGION` — paper-cream on a near-black `mighty.shadow` painted-concrete ground, lit by a single hard top-down spotlight gradient (small-source key, the Rocky look). Chalk-dust motes in the light. No image; the type *is* the image.
- **Naming:** Unbranded creed.

### 03 · 120 → 78 / FORGED AT 40 (the transformation wall)
- **Type / size:** Wall poster pair or one wide panel, landscape 16:9 wall-scale.
- **Headline:** `120 → 78`
- **Sub-copy:** `FORGED AT 40.` (line two) · small line three: `NO MAGIC PLAN. I CHOSE TO SHOW UP.`
- **Decal/footer:** mono, lower-left: `· 7–8 MONTHS · THEN I NEVER STOPPED ·`
- **Treatment:** Archetype C. Huge stencil numerals `120 → 78` in paper-cream with rust streaks bleeding down, the arrow hand-painted heavy. Ground is half painted-concrete dark, half torn paper-cream — the split telling the before/after. `FORGED AT 40` stamped beneath in brass.
- **Naming:** Unbranded story (his numbers). Tiny eGym footer optional.

### 04 · SHOW UP
- **Type / size:** Minimal huge wall-wording, landscape, mounted high over the entrance or above the mirrors.
- **Headline:** `SHOW UP.`
- **Sub-copy:** none.
- **Decal/footer:** none.
- **Treatment:** Archetype A, minimalist. Two words, the largest type in the building, paper-cream painted straight onto raw brick with a stencil edge and spray-overspray so it looks sprayed by hand at 5am. The period is deliberate — an instruction, not a slogan. This is the first and last thing every member reads.
- **Naming:** Unbranded creed.

### 18 · WEBSITE HERO / THEME TREATMENT (the system online)
- **Type / size:** Direction note — how the same environment themes the live site (`robincarruthers.com` / eGym pages).
- **Headline (site hero):** the `eGym` canvas banner up top; the section heroes reuse `IRON IS RELIGION`, `120 → 78`, `I AM A WARRIOR`, `SHOW UP` as scroll chapters — the wall posters, made of pixels.
- **Sub-copy:** site decals reuse the mono rhythm `· eGym · LOKHANDWALA · ANDHERI W · MUMBAI ·`.
- **Treatment & rules (so on-site and on-wall are one brand):**
  - **Backgrounds:** brick photo for hero/dark sections (`bg-brick` + warm vignette), paper-cream for light sections, painted-concrete `mighty.shadow` for creed sections — exactly the wall materials.
  - **Hero =** the eGym canvas banner on brick (~1° tilt, two bolts, frayed edge), Robin's photo as a torn-edge pinned poster beside it.
  - **Type:** Alfa Slab One headlines (paint-bleed filter), JetBrains Mono decals at wide tracking, Inter body. Never pure white — always `rocky.paper`.
  - **Color discipline:** `mighty.red` + brass + leather warmth; ring-blue exactly once per viewport (the boxing/CTA accent), mirroring the one-cool-accent wall rule.
  - **Motion:** slow heavy reveals (~1200ms) for creed lines, short punch hits (~300ms) for stamps; honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. The site should feel like walking the gym floor — A-B-C archetype rhythm down the scroll.
  - **Naming:** **eGym-named** in nav/hero/footer; creed chapters stay name-light, same as the walls. Wallpapers (16–17) are downloadable from the site.
