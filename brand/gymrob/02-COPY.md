# GYMROB — Final Copy

> Copy deliverable for the Robin Carruthers gift identity. Voice = hand-painted
> gym wall, fight-card, brass plaque. Old-school iron, Mumbai. Gritty, warm,
> earned — never corporate-clean. Nothing invented beyond what the site gives us.
>
> Convention: lines in `MONO CAPS` are stamped/metadata (JetBrains Mono),
> lines in **SLAB** are painted/engraved hero (Alfa Slab One), `· dots ·` are
> the spacer rhythm. The arrow is always `120 → 78`.

---

## 1. CABIN NAMEPLATE — the hero deliverable

### The decision

**RECOMMENDATION: `CHIEF IRON OFFICER` — locked.**

Pressure-test. The job is one title that does three things at once: winks at the
adland C-suite he walked away from, reads as pure iron, and survives engraved at
small size. Ranked the shortlist against that:

- **CHIEF IRON OFFICER** — the `C.I.O.` pun is the whole joke and it's a *good*
  one: a man who spent 30 years being a Chief-something in boardrooms is now
  Chief Iron Officer of a gym. Three hard words, three hard syllables, engraves
  beautifully. It carries his past and his present in one breath. **Winner.**
- *CHIEF WARRIOR OFFICER* — strong, but "warrior" is already doing the talking on
  the tee and the wall. Don't spend it here.
- *KEEPER OF THE IRON* — gorgeous, monastic, very "iron is religion" — but it
  drops the C-suite wink entirely. Holding it as the runner-up and as a side mark.
- *CHIEF REINVENTION OFFICER* — clever (120→78, reinvented at 40) but a touch
  explainy and HR-deck. The story should be felt, not labelled.
- *HEAD COACH & FOUNDER* — true and useful, but it's the honest-job-title version,
  not the cool one. Keep it for the business card / lanyard, not the plate.

**Alternatives if a second opinion is wanted (in order):**
1. `KEEPER OF THE IRON`
2. `CHIEF WARRIOR OFFICER`
3. `IRON OFFICER IN CHIEF`

### Desk plate (brass-on-walnut) — 3-line layout

```
            · CHIEF IRON OFFICER ·            ← small role line, MONO CAPS, brass

            ROBIN  CARRUTHERS                 ← big, engraved brass slab

         · MUMBAI · IRON IS RELIGION ·        ← tiny support line, MONO CAPS
```

- Line A (role): `· CHIEF IRON OFFICER ·` — mono, wide tracking, small.
- Line B (name): **ROBIN CARRUTHERS** — the hero. Largest, engraved brass slab.
- Line C (support): `· MUMBAI · IRON IS RELIGION ·` — mono, smallest.
- **No organisation name. Hard rule honoured.**

### Cabin-door plate (taller / more presence)

```
              · CHIEF IRON OFFICER ·

                ROBIN  CARRUTHERS

          · 120 → 78 · OLD-SCHOOL IRON ·
```

Same three-line grammar; the door version swaps the support line to
`· 120 → 78 · OLD-SCHOOL IRON ·` so the transformation reads as you walk in.
(Keep `IRON IS RELIGION` for the desk where coffee gets set down; keep
`120 → 78` for the door where the world first sees him.)

the comeback. Heavier, more personal — best as the second colourway.

explaining any of it. This is the line on the plate, the polo, the helmet, the
