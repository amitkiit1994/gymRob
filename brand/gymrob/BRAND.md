# BRANDSCAPE · GYMROB

A complete brand identity & gift set for **Robin Carruthers** — Mumbai's old-school
iron coach, the real-life Rocky. From the boardrooms of adland to the gym floor:
**120 → 78, forged at 40.** *Iron is religion.*

> "Brandscape" = the landscape of the brand — the mark, and the mark put to work
> across everything he carries. Built as a gift.

---

## 1. The mark system

GYMROB runs on **three forms + one fist**, drawn from a competitive concept sprint
(7 designers) and a 4-lens creative-director panel that converged on this synthesis:

| Form | File | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary lockup** | `logo/lockup-horizontal.svg` | letterheads, site header, hero |
| **Painted wordmark** | `logo/wordmark.svg` | tees, tote, towel, banner |
| **Warrior's seal (emblem)** | `logo/emblem.svg` | patch, coaster, pin, stamps |
| **GR monogram (icon)** | `logo/icon-gr.svg` | cap, polo chest, favicon, bike badge |
| **The fist** | `logo/fist.svg` | the personal symbol — stickers, pins, decals |
| **Favicon** | `logo/favicon.svg` | 16–48px |
| 1-colour knockouts | `logo/emblem-mono.svg`, `logo/icon-gr-mono.svg` | embroidery / emboss / die-cut |
| Hero banner | `logo/banner.svg` | the Mighty-Mick's wall sign |

**Why the fist:** it's Robin's own "fists up" moment beside Sylvester Stallone — the
single most personal symbol he owns. It is the heart of the system.

### Rules
1. **Two inks + one cool tick.** `mighty.red` + `rocky.paper`, with `mighty.shadow`
   as the dark. `ring-blue` appears **once** per composition, never more.
2. **Never pure white** — always `rocky.paper #fefae0`.
3. **Squared.** Max 2px corner radius. (A circular *seal* is allowed.)
4. **Three jobs, three forms.** Wordmark carries the voice; the seal carries the
   heart; the GR carries the small sizes (24px-safe). Don't force one to do all.
5. **1-colour safe.** Every mark ships as a single-ink knockout for stitch/emboss/
   die-cut. Distress/hammer is seasoning, never structure.
6. Production: outline the live-text marks before manufacture.

## 3. Type — 2 + 1
- **Alfa Slab One** — painted signage, the wordmark.
- **JetBrains Mono** — stamped IDs, decals, ring type.
- **Inter** — body.

(All three are the site's own fonts — the brandscape matches the live site exactly.)
